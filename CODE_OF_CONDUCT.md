# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in the AgriSignal community a harassment-free, respectful, and inclusive experience for everyone—especially smallholder farmer representatives, agronomists, extension officers, and software engineers across East Africa and globally.

## Our Standards

- Use welcoming and inclusive language across English, Luganda, Swahili, and technical discussions.
- Respect differing agronomic perspectives and regional smallholder farming constraints.
- Never publish unverified chemical pesticide prescriptions or misleading yield guarantees.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers at `codesphereinnovationz@gmail.com`.
